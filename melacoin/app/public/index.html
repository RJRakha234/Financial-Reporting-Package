M MelaCoin

Loyalty points that don't die in one shop

Every shop decides how many points a rupee earns and what a point is worth. Customers spend those points at that shop — or turn them into MelaCoin, one token that works across the whole network.

1Shop sets the rulese.g. 2 points per ₹1, 1 point = ₹0.05
2Customer buysPoints land in their account automatically
3Redeem or upgradeUse points at that shop, or convert to MelaCoin
4MelaCoin travelsSpend at any shop, or move it to your own wallet
