# Indore — 2-Day Trip Itinerary

**Dates:** Mon, 15 June 2026 → Tue, 16 June 2026
**Season note:** Mid-June is early monsoon — waterfalls are reviving and the countryside is green. Carry an umbrella/raincoat and quick-dry footwear.

**Already covered (skipped here):** Sarafa Market, Omkareshwar Mandir, Omkareshwar boating.

> ⚠️ **Monday closures:** Lal Bagh Palace, Rajwada (interior/museum), and the Central Museum are **closed on Mondays**. They're scheduled for Day 2 (Tuesday) instead.

---

## Day 1 — Monday, 15 June (Temples, Monsoon Waterfalls & Street Food)

| Time | Plan |
|------|------|
| 8:00 AM | Breakfast — **poha-jalebi** at *Chappan Dukan* or a local joint (the Indore classic) |
| 9:00 AM | **Khajrana Ganesh Temple** — Indore's most revered temple, best visited early before crowds |
| 10:15 AM | **Bada Ganpati** — home to one of the largest Ganesha idols in the world |
| 11:00 AM | **Kanch Mandir (Glass Temple)** — a stunning Jain temple inlaid entirely with mirror & glasswork |
| 12:30 PM | Drive toward **Patalpani** (~35 km, ~1 hr) |
| 1:00 PM | Lunch en route / near Mhow |
| 2:00 PM | **Patalpani Waterfall** — spectacular in monsoon (~300 ft). *View from designated safe points only — do not go near the edge.* |
| 4:00 PM | **Tincha Falls** (nearby) — quieter, scenic monsoon spot (optional) |
| 6:30 PM | Back to city — relax / freshen up |
| 7:30 PM | Dinner & evening at **Chappan Dukan** (56 shops) — shikanji, garadu, sandwiches, hot dogs, chaat |

---

## Day 2 — Tuesday, 16 June (Heritage Morning + Ujjain Excursion)

**Recommended: Heritage morning in Indore + spiritual day trip to Ujjain**

| Time | Plan |
|------|------|
| 7:00 AM | Early breakfast |
| 7:30 AM | **Rajwada Palace** — the historic 7-storey Holkar palace in the old city |
| 8:30 AM | **Lal Bagh Palace** — grand Holkar-era palace with European interiors and gardens |
| 10:00 AM | Drive to **Ujjain** (~55 km, ~1.5 hrs) |
| 11:30 AM | **Mahakaleshwar Jyotirling** — one of the 12 Jyotirlingas, Ujjain's holiest shrine. *(Tip: book a darshan/Bhasma Aarti slot in advance to skip long queues.)* |
| 1:30 PM | Lunch in Ujjain (try local thali / *dal-bafla*) |
| 2:30 PM | **Kal Bhairav Temple** — famous for its unique liquor-offering ritual |
| 3:30 PM | **Ram Ghat** on the Shipra River — serene riverside ghats (site of the Kumbh/Simhastha) |
| 4:30 PM | **Sandipani Ashram** — the legendary school where Krishna & Sudama studied |
| 5:15 PM | **Mangalnath Temple** (optional) — considered the birthplace of Mars (Mangal) |
| 6:00 PM | Drive back to Indore (~1.5 hrs) |
| 8:00 PM | Farewell dinner — Indore thali (try *Apna Sweets* or *Guru Kripa*) |

**Alternative (Lighter, city-only day):**
Rajwada → Lal Bagh Palace → Central Museum → Annapurna Temple → ISKCON Temple → Kamla Nehru Zoo → evening shopping at Treasure Island / C21 Mall.

## Quick Tips
- **Getting around:** Hire a cab/self-drive for Day 1 (waterfalls) and Day 2 (Ujjain). Within the city, autos and Ola/Uber work well.
- **Ujjain darshan:** Pre-book a Mahakaleshwar darshan slot online; the Bhasma Aarti (very early morning) needs advance booking if you want to attend it.
- **Must-eat in Indore:** Poha-jalebi, garadu (fried yam), bhutte ka kees, shikanji (thick), khopra patties, and dahi bada.
- **Monsoon safety:** Waterfall points can be slippery and currents strong — stay behind barriers.
- **Timing buffers:** Add cushion on Day 2 for the Ujjain drive and temple queues.

Enjoy Indore! 🌧️🛕🍽️
